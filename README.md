### What is TAGit?

**TAGit** is a free, browser-based watermark studio.  
Upload your logo + any image, choose a position, tweak size / opacity / rotation, and download the tagged version — ready to post.

It’s never been easier to watermark or tag your content before you share it.

**100% Free · No sign-up · Files stay in your browser**

---

### How it works

1. **Upload Logo** — PNG, SVG or WebP (transparent works best)
2. **Upload Media** — Your image (JPG / PNG / WebP…)
3. **Position & Style** — 9-point grid + size, opacity, rotation, padding, tiling & shadow
4. **Live Preview** — Real-time canvas preview
5. **Download** — Get your watermarked file instantly

---

### Features

- Drag & drop upload zones
- Live canvas preview
- 9 position presets (corners, edges & center)
- Size, opacity, rotation & padding controls
- Tiled watermark option

- Fully client-side (your files never leave the browser)
